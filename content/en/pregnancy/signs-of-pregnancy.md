## Glance
A missed period is often the first sign of pregnancy, sometimes alongside nausea, tiredness or tender breasts.

## Overview
For many people the first hint of pregnancy is a **missed period**. But bodies vary, and some notice
other early signs first — often around 4 to 6 weeks in.

Common early signs include feeling or being sick (at any time of day, not just the morning), breasts
that feel larger or tender, feeling unusually tired, and needing to pee more often. Some people also
notice a metallic taste, a new sensitivity to certain smells, or going off foods they normally like.

None of these on their own means you're definitely pregnant — many overlap with the ordinary ups and
downs of the cycle — and not everyone gets them. The way to know for sure is a pregnancy test.

## When to see a doctor
- If a test is positive, contact a **midwife or GP** to start your pregnancy (antenatal) care early.
- If your period is late and tests are negative but symptoms continue, it's worth checking in with a
  GP to look at what else might be going on.
