## Glance
In the days before her period she may feel more tired, tender or low — meet it with patience, not jokes.

## Overview
In the week or so before a period, many people get premenstrual symptoms: mood changes, irritability
or feeling low, tiredness, sore breasts, bloating, headaches, or trouble sleeping. It's very common
and it's real — driven by the natural hormone shift of the luteal phase, not by her "overreacting".

For a smaller number of people the low mood is severe (a condition called PMDD) and deserves real
support and medical care. Either way, your job isn't to diagnose or fix her — it's to be steady and
kind.

The single most damaging thing a partner can do here is weaponise it: "you're just PMSing", eye-rolls,
or treating her feelings as invalid because of the date. That dismisses her and adds hurt to a hard
week. Her feelings still count, even when hormones are part of the picture.

## Deep
What actually helps in these days:

- **Lighten her load.** Take on chores, cooking, or the mental to-do list without being asked.
- **Comfort, not correction.** Offer warmth, rest, a hot water bottle, her favourite food — and let
  her feel what she feels.
- **Listen first.** If she's upset, "that sounds really hard" beats "calm down" every time.
- **Don't keep score.** A harder day isn't a debt she owes you later.
- **Ask what she needs** — some want closeness, some want space. Both are okay; respect her answer.
