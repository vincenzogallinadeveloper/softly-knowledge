## Glance
Consent is a clear, free, enthusiastic yes — every time, and it can be withdrawn at any point.

## Overview
Respect starts with her right to decide about her own body — always. Consent to intimacy means a
freely given, informed, enthusiastic **yes**. Silence isn't consent. A reluctant "fine" isn't consent.
Someone who's asleep, very drunk, or under pressure can't truly consent. And a yes to one thing, or on
one day, isn't a yes to everything or to next time.

Consent can be withdrawn at any moment, and "stop" or "not tonight" is a complete answer that needs no
justification. Pressuring, guilt-tripping, sulking until she gives in, or treating sex as something
you're owed are not okay — a relationship isn't a debt.

## Deep
What respect looks like in practice:

- **Ask, and check in.** "Is this okay?" and paying attention to how she responds — words and body.
- **Take "no" gracefully.** No pouting, pressure, or punishment. Her answer is the end of it.
- **Enthusiasm matters.** If she seems unsure, hesitant or just going along with it, stop and ask.
- **Her body, her call** — about intimacy, contraception, pregnancy, her health, her appearance, her
  choices. You get a voice in your shared life, never a veto over her body.
- **Respect is daily.** How you speak to her, about her, and treat her boundaries in small things is
  the foundation everything else rests on.
