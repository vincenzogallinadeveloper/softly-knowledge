## Glance
Stopping smoking is one of the best things you can do for your baby — and it's never too late.

## Overview
Stopping smoking is one of the most powerful things you can do to give your baby a healthy start.
Every cigarette restricts the oxygen your baby gets, which makes their heart work harder and can
hold back their growth.

Smoking in pregnancy raises the risk of serious problems, including miscarriage, premature birth,
low birth weight, stillbirth, and sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS) after the birth. Second-hand
smoke matters too, so a smoke-free home helps.

The encouraging part: it's **never too late**. Stopping at any point in pregnancy helps, and quitting
early brings the most benefit. It can be genuinely hard, which is exactly why free help exists — and
using it makes success much more likely.

## When to see a doctor
- Ask your **midwife** about **free NHS stop-smoking support** — trained advisers (some who
  specialise in pregnancy) can support you, and there's a Smokefree helpline too. Getting help is a
  sign of looking after your baby, not a failing.
