## Glance
Feeling your baby move is a sign they're well — if the movements slow or change, call your midwife straight away.

## Overview
Feeling your baby move is one of the reassuring signs that they're doing well. Most people start to
feel movements somewhere between 16 and 24 weeks of pregnancy — often later in a first pregnancy, so
after 20 weeks is common the first time around.

There's no set number of movements you "should" feel, and no need to count kicks against a target.
Every baby is different, and what matters is getting to know **your** baby's own usual pattern
through the day — the times they're active and the times they're quiet.

Once you know that pattern, a change from it is the thing to pay attention to. Trust what you know
about your own baby.

## When to see a doctor
Getting to know your baby's normal pattern is what lets you notice a change. If you're ever unsure
whether movements have slowed or changed, it is always right to call and check — you will never be
wasting anyone's time.

## Red flags
- If your baby is **moving less than usual, their movements change, or you can't feel them moving**,
  **call your midwife or maternity unit immediately** — at any time of day or night. **Do not wait
  until the next day**, even if it's the middle of the night.
- Do not rely on a home heartbeat monitor (doppler) or an app to reassure yourself — get checked in
  person.
