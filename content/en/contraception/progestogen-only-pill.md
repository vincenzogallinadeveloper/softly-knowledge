## Glance
The mini pill is a daily pill with one hormone that thickens cervical mucus to block sperm.

## Overview
The progestogen-only pill — often called the mini pill — is a daily tablet containing just one
hormone, a progestogen. It's an option for people who can't or would rather not take estrogen.

Its main action is to thicken the mucus at the neck of the womb, so sperm find it harder to reach an
egg. One common type, the desogestrel pill, also stops the ovaries from releasing an egg
(ovulation).

Taken correctly, it's over 99% effective; with typical everyday use it's around 91%. As with other
pills, timing matters, and it doesn't protect against STIs.

## Deep
The mini pill differs from the combined pill in having no estrogen, which changes who it may suit —
something a clinician weighs up when it's prescribed. Some types need to be taken within a fairly
tight daily window to stay effective. This atom covers what it is and how it works; how to take it
and whether it's right for you are matters for a GP, nurse, or pharmacist.
