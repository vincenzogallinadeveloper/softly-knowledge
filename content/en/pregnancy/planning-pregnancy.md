## Glance
A few simple steps before trying — folic acid, stopping smoking and alcohol — give a pregnancy the best start.

## Overview
If you're thinking about trying for a baby, a handful of steps beforehand can give a pregnancy a
healthier start — many of them working from before you even conceive.

The main ones: start taking **folic acid** (and vitamin D), **stop smoking**, and **avoid alcohol**.
Aiming for a healthy weight helps too, as being significantly under- or over-weight can make it
harder to conceive. It's also worth making sure your **MMR vaccination** is up to date, since some
infections can affect a pregnancy.

None of this needs to be perfect or stressful. Small, kind changes ahead of time are what matter —
and getting a bit of a head start is one of the nicest things you can do for a future pregnancy.

## When to see a doctor
- If you have a **long-term health condition** or take **regular medicine**, see your GP before you
  start trying — some conditions and medicines need planning around. **Don't stop any prescribed
  medicine** without talking to your doctor first.
- A GP can also advise on folic acid (including whether you need a higher dose) and getting to a
  healthy weight before pregnancy.
